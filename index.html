I am an environmental and resource economics researcher focusing on environmental compliance, wastewater regulation, weather–hydrology interactions, and critical mineral supply chains. My work combines econometric analysis with big data analytics, spatial data integration, geospatial modeling, and environmental data engineering to examine complex environmental and policy challenges.

Research Interests

Environmental and Resource Economics, Environmental Compliance, Water Quality Regulation, Critical Mineral Supply Chains, Big Data Analytics, Spatial Data Integration, Geospatial Modeling, Environmental Data Engineering, Applied Econometrics, and Public Policy.
